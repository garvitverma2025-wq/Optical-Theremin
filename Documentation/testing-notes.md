# Testing Notes

## Prototype observations

- Breadboard implementation assembled.
- Audible output was observed during testing with a two-wire buzzer.
- The buzzer produced a squealing tone.
- The available observation does not by itself prove that pitch continuously tracks hand position.

## Further verification

1. Confirm the NE555 is in the intended astable configuration.
2. Measure or observe pin 3 frequency while changing illumination on the LDR.
3. Verify that frequency changes repeatably as the LDR is shaded and illuminated.
4. Test the LM386 output with an 8 Ω speaker and correctly polarized coupling capacitor.
5. Record measured frequencies, supply voltage, and component values.

Do not describe continuous pitch tracking as verified until these checks have been completed.
